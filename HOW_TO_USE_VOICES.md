# 🎙️ How to Choose Eleven Labs Voices

## 📱 **In the Mobile App**

### **Step 1: Open the App**
- Connect to Expo Go using: `exp://21.0.0.12:8081`
- Or scan the QR code

### **Step 2: Select a Voice**
1. Look at the **top of the screen**
2. You'll see two buttons next to the app title:
   - **🎙 Microphone icon** - Voice selector
   - **ESV/NKJV button** - Bible version

3. **Tap the 🎙 microphone icon**

### **Step 3: Browse Voices**
A beautiful modal will appear showing all 8 voices:

| Voice | Description | Best For |
|-------|-------------|----------|
| **Adam** | Deep, authoritative male voice | Narratives, Historical books, Genesis |
| **Rachel** | Clear, calm female voice | Psalms, Poetry, Proverbs |
| **Arnold** | Mature, resonant male voice | Gospels, New Testament letters |
| **Antoni** | Well-rounded, versatile male | General reading |
| **Domi** | Strong, confident female | Prophets, Strong passages |
| **Bella** | Soft, gentle female | Devotional, Peaceful passages |
| **Josh** | Young, energetic male | Youth-oriented passages |
| **Sam** | Clear, dynamic male | General reading |

### **Step 4: Choose Your Voice**
1. **Tap on any voice** to select it
2. The modal will close automatically
3. Your selection is saved!

### **Step 5: Listen**
1. Navigate to any Bible chapter
2. **Tap the Play button** ▶ (big purple button in the center)
3. The text will be read in your selected voice!

---

## 🎛️ **Audio Controls**

### **Playback Controls:**
- **▶/⏸ Center button** - Play or Pause
- **⏮ Left button** - Previous chapter
- **⏭ Right button** - Next chapter

### **Speed Control:**
- Tap **"Speed: 1x"** button at the bottom
- Cycles through:
  - 0.75x (slower)
  - 1x (normal)
  - 1.25x (faster)
  - 1.5x (fastest)

### **Progress Bar:**
- Shows current position in the audio
- Displays time: `0:15 / 3:42`

---

## 💡 **Tips**

### **Best Voices for Different Books:**

**Old Testament:**
- Genesis, Exodus, Numbers: **Adam** or **Arnold**
- Psalms: **Rachel** or **Bella**
- Proverbs, Ecclesiastes: **Rachel**
- Prophets (Isaiah, Jeremiah): **Domi** or **Arnold**

**New Testament:**
- Gospels: **Arnold** or **Adam**
- Acts: **Antoni** or **Sam**
- Letters (Romans, Corinthians): **Arnold**
- Revelation: **Adam** or **Arnold**

### **For Different Moods:**
- **Calming/Meditative**: Rachel, Bella
- **Authoritative/Powerful**: Adam, Arnold
- **Clear/Educational**: Sam, Antoni
- **Energetic**: Josh

---

## 🔄 **Changing Voices Mid-Chapter**

You can change voices anytime:
1. **Tap the 🎙 icon** while audio is playing
2. **Select a new voice**
3. **Tap Play** again
4. The same chapter will play in the new voice!
